The ASA | Bullhorn AI Job Impact is Bullhorn’s latest research, launched in partnership with the American Staffing Association, to show AI’s real impact on the U.S. job market. Updated monthly, the report uses real hiring data to measure how AI is affecting different job categories based on hiring activity.
