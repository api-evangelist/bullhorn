The summer hiring surge drove growth across most sectors, but what’s behind that growth reveals important shifts in where opportunities are emerging. Healthcare hiring jumped 10%, while retail and hospitality grew 9%, driven largely by gains in hospitality. While these increases align with typical seasonal patterns, year-over-year comparisons tell a different story.
