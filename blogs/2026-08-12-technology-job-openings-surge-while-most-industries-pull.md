June’s job market tells two stories: Job openings declined across nearly every industry, reversing May’s momentum, while technology surged 6.5% and construction outperformed seasonal trends. At the same time, salary growth accelerated in key sectors, reinforcing that labor demand is becoming increasingly uneven. For staffing firms, this fragmentation means specialization is no longer optional.
