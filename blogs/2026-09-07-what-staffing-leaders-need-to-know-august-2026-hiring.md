Together, Bullhorn’s latest Hiring Outlook and Job Market Trends August data paints a picture of an uneven hiring market. Permanent hiring remains resilient while temporary job orders are slowing. Most industries saw fewer job openings in August, but manufacturing and construction were the exceptions to that trend.
